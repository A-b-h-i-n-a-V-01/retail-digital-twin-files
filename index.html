🎯 Section 01
Objectives of the Project
🏪
Virtual Retail Digital Twin
Build a virtual mirror of a retail store using historical and real-time data to simulate business conditions and forecast outcomes before they occur in the real world.
📈
Intelligent Forecasting Engine
Integrate PatchTST (Transformer-based time-series) for daily/weekly sales demand prediction and XGBoost for inventory restock quantity and timing optimization.
👥
Customer Intelligence
Perform K-Means customer segmentation into Premium / Loyal / New / At-Risk profiles, churn prediction, footfall forecasting, and BERT sentiment analysis on reviews.
🛒
Smart Recommendation System
Suggest frequently bought-together products using Collaborative Filtering and Market Basket Analysis (Apriori / FP-Growth) to increase average order value.
🤖
Generative AI Business Copilot
Deploy LLM (Llama 3 / Gemma) to answer natural-language business queries — "Should I restock Product X?" — and auto-generate daily/weekly business reports.
🔮
What-If Simulation
Enable managers to simulate festival surges, pricing changes, and inventory variations in the Digital Twin before committing to real-world decisions.

🔍 Section 03
Gaps Identified
🤱
Fragmented Solutions
Existing retail tools address one domain at a time. No unified intelligent platform integrates forecasting, segmentation, sentiment, recommendations and simulation for SMBs.
🔮
No What-If Simulation
Retailers cannot test decisions virtually. No SMB tool offers a Digital Twin sandbox to simulate festivals, stock-outs, or pricing changes before real-world execution.
🤖
Missing GenAI Copilot
BI dashboards are static and require expert interpretation. No system provides a conversational LLM copilot that answers natural-language business questions in real time.
💰
Cost Barrier for SMBs
Enterprise platforms like SAP and Oracle are inaccessible to small/medium retailers due to prohibitive licensing costs. Open-source alternatives lack predictive intelligence.
📊
Reactive, Not Proactive
Current tools analyze past performance only. They lack proactive predictive capabilities that help managers act before stockouts, churn, or revenue drops materialize.
📝
Manual Reporting
Retail managers compile reports manually every week. Automated AI report generation from multi-domain analytics remains an open research problem for the retail sector.
